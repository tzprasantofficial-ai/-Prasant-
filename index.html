01 — ABOUT
THE SHORT VERSION

I'm Prasant — raised in Shillong, built in the UAE. My road ran through airport gates with Bangkok Airways, call floors in Dubai and nine-plus years of face-to-face sales, and every stop taught me the same thing: people don't buy products, they buy stories. Today I tell those stories with video, motion graphics and design — directing AI tools like a small production crew to turn any brief into finished, on-brand work. No coding required: I direct AI the way a director runs a shoot, and the results are real.

Off the clock you'll find me heat-pressing shirts at U-Design, building QR tech for Al Ain's shops, teaching UAE businesses AI through Aakriti — or on final approach in a virtual A320. Fluent in English, Hindi, Nepali and Urdu. Powered by curiosity and cutting-edge AI.

08 — MY LIFE · MY PEOPLE

Fourteen Cats & Counting

None of them were planned. Every one of them is family. I'm the guy who eats chicken yet can't meet the eyes of a goat in a transport truck without whispering sorry — compassion isn't logical, it's inherited. My family have been givers and animal people since before I could walk. Here's how a small studio in Al Ain became a full house, chapter by chapter.

01
Chapter 01 — Where it began

Coco

New studio, living alone, thinking about getting a pet — then I found a small cat inside my neighbour's place and rescued her, sure she'd wandered in by mistake. Named her Coco after serious research into the best cat names. Her spay surgery later terrified me — I was scared of losing her — but we both came out stronger, and life restarted.
